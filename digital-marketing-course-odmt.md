## What Is Digital Marketing?

Digital marketing is the process of promoting products or services using online channels such as search engines, social media, websites, email, and paid advertisements. Unlike traditional marketing, digital marketing allows businesses to track performance, measure results, and optimize campaigns in real time.

With the growing use of the internet and smartphones, digital marketing has become an essential skill for businesses of all sizes.
